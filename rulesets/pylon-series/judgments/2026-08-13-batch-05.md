# Batch 05 — the first dark batch

8 pieces (085–092), judged 2026-08-13 from the drafts. The first batch sampled
entirely under the rules the color campaign produced (`rules_sha 9abacd67518a`), and
the first to reach the dusk-dark territory the old mood bands forbade.

| piece | verdict | note |
|---|---|---|
| 085–092 | holds | "난 배치 괜찮은것 같아." |

**What the distillation bought.** 089 (snowfield, slate-dusk) is the darkest picture
in the series: the field sinks to near-black and the tower survives as silhouette
alone. That anchor lightness — 0.38–0.65 — was outside what the rules permitted two
days ago. 088 lands the ashen band, chroma stripped to almost nothing, which is the
exact register the campaign's vision judge scored *low* and the author scores high;
the map's one exposed bias, now standing as a piece. The campaign's verdict about
where the fertile ground lay reproduced in production.

**Open, second sighting: the head-on line.** 086 (mountains) puts the camera square
on the axis of the line — tower dead-centre, conductors sweeping away in mirror
symmetry, the valley barely reading behind it. Nothing in the rules forbids it; the
frustum check is satisfied by a head-on aim as readily as an oblique one. This is the
same observation carried out of batch 03 (055) and batch 04's batch-wide note, which
is what makes it worth recording rather than shrugging at: three batches running, the
strictly colinear vocabulary keeps producing the one composition that shows it off.

Not acted on in this batch. It belongs with the angle-tower revision already queued
in "Not yet variables" — but there is a cheaper move available first, and the two
should be weighed together rather than in sequence: a camera-obliqueness rule costs a
threshold in `checks.json`, while line bends cost the straight-line assumption
threaded through the frustum check, the terrain corridor, and the wire chain. Neither
was chosen here; the question is which one the next campaign should ask about.
