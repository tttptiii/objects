**Batch-wide note.** "그 외에는 다 너무 전신주들이 일직선상에 있는 것 같아서 약간
아쉬워" — every line in the vocabulary is strictly colinear, and across a batch that
reads as monotony. This is the angle-tower (line bend) revision, already listed under
"Not yet variables" as a structural change (the straight-line assumption runs through
the frustum check, the terrain corridor, and the wire chain). This judgment moves it
from "candidate for later" to next in line.

Identified this session, same mechanism: the "dark horizon band" flagged at the
graphic-identity decision (039, 067, 079, 083) is the hedgerow too — one feature,
two readings (crack line near, broken band far).

**The crack, tried and declined (same day).** The hedgerow was generalized into a
"crack" — a band available on any land, seated on the terrain, exempt from fog,
colored to collide (sky complement at near-maximal chroma) — and demoed on all
three kinds. The author declined the whole direction: "그냥 기존처럼 균열이
우연히 있는 것이 나은 것 같고, 색이 없는 것이 나은 것 같아." The hedgerow stays
what it was — incidental, dark, farmland's own. Renderer support reverted
(`b33e962` → `d6cf8f0`); the `hedge_color` promotion is dropped, and the horizon
band stands as accepted. The principle underneath is worth keeping: this series
prefers what the rules happen to produce over accents placed for effect.
